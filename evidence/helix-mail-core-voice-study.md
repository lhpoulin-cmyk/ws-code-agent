# helix-mail-core voice study

Read-only precedent study. `helix-mail-core` was not modified.

Inspected: `docs/README.md`, `docs/architecture.md`,
`docs/security-boundary.md`, `docs/known-limitations.md`,
`docs/release-status.md`, `evidence/README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and
representative implementation/evidence records.

The strongest precedent separates public narrative, architecture/policy,
runbooks, implementation packets, and evidence. It puts status and limits
plainly, keeps evidence in its recorded tense, and uses factual changelog
addenda rather than rewriting historical packets. Its rule that a joke is not
a rollback method is a useful boundary: readable narrative may have voice,
but safety instructions and evidence remain precise.

Mail-specific Fastmail, Stalwart, JMAP, SMTP, `home.arpa`, mailbox, and Katra
policy are not generalized here. The resulting application voice is a
provenance-informed adaptation, not a copy of mail-domain doctrine.
