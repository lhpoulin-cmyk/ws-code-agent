# Doc Writer conversational proposal v2 — shared voice contract

Use one shared portfolio voice: direct, intelligent, evidence-first, natural,
exact without performing expertise, clear about status and uncertainty, and
willing to state the real point. Preserve established technical terminology.

Do not sound bureaucratic, corporate, academic, consultant-like, or like an
assistant performing helpfulness. Humor is allowed only where harmless; never
use humor in safety, destructive, incident, or acceptance language. Later
audience adaptation may change presentation without changing meaning.

This contract does not claim to know the operator's personal voice. Operator-
specific tone learning is outside this phase.
